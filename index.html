About Us

CERBOAR is a metal band playing at the intersection of Groove, Sludge, Stoner, and Thrash Metal.

What does our name mean? It's a symbiosis of Cerberus (guarding the gates of hell) and the word BOAR. Conceptually, we are a three-headed infernal boar feeding on beer and posers' souls.

Currently, we are generating the heaviest riffs possible and are in the process of creating our debut album.
